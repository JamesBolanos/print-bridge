# Disclaimer — Non-Affiliation

## Purpose

print-bridge is a vendor-neutral local print bridge. To avoid trademark confusion or any implication of endorsement/support from any printer manufacturer, this disclaimer must appear everywhere the product is named or shown, per BR-7 in `01-business/brd.md` and referenced from `05-ux/tray-app-spec.md` and `08-backlog/stories.md`.

## Standard text (long form — repo README, install/landing docs)

> print-bridge is an independent, open project created to provide a free bridge for browser-to-printer communication. print-bridge is **not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or in any way officially connected with any printer manufacturer**. Product names, marks, and logos are trademarks of their respective owners and are referenced only for descriptive compatibility context.

## Short form (in-app, main window)

> print-bridge is an independent project, not affiliated with or endorsed by any printer manufacturer.

## Usage notes

- The short form is sufficient for space-constrained UI (main window, tray menu tooltip if one is ever added). The long form should appear at least once in any public-facing document (README, download page).
- Do not use any printer manufacturer's logo, product imagery, or stylized branding anywhere in print-bridge's UI, marketing, or documentation.
- If the project's name or branding is ever revised, re-check this disclaimer for continued accuracy.
- This is general guidance for a solo/free project, not a substitute for legal advice. If the project grows into anything commercial, revisit this with an actual trademark/IP-aware review rather than relying on this document alone.
