To avoid trademark confusion or any implication of endorsement/support from Zebra Technologies (or any other printer manufacturer), this disclaimer must appear everywhere the product is named or shown, per BR-7 in `01-business/brd.md` and referenced from `05-ux/tray-app-spec.md` (S3.1) and `08-backlog/stories.md` (S6.1).

- The short form is sufficient for space-constrained UI (main window, tray menu tooltip if one is ever added). The long form should appear at least once in any public-facing document (README, download page).
